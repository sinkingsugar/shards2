## 2026-10-05-a003cb7-claude-3dc841#F1
- Disposition: fixed (implementation claim, awaiting independent verification).
- Change/reason: both flow loops now move the previous output into `inline::run`. It starts in owned scratch. Const/Get/Set/Inc clear obsolete scratch after reanchoring to code/frame storage; Take/Push/generic arithmetic replace it with their result. Typed numeric operations can leave only resource-free numeric scratch, avoiding general cleanup work per arithmetic operation. Scratch output is moved out at the boundary; other outputs are cloned into an owned snapshot. No pointer escapes and generic shard signatures are unchanged.
- Regression evidence: `discarded_inline_input_does_not_copy_captured_sequence` checks Arc allocation identity and final contents after constructor → Const → Push → Pause, for sequence/table constructors on both schedulers. On the original a003cb7 runtime, the new shared test failed on both backends (each at its first sequence case). All cases pass after the fix, natively and on WASI. The identity assertion detects the unnecessary copy without a timing threshold.
- Additional coverage: `obsolete_input_and_take_scratch_are_released_before_push` covers Const and Get replacements, with and without intermediate Take scratch; `owned_input_survives_passthrough_and_self_push` covers retained output and self-push snapshots. Existing differential, aliasing, overflow and saved-output tests remain passing. All seven inline executor tests passed Miri on nightly-2026-10-03.
- Performance evidence: the exact review patterns were measured at 10,000 and 40,000 iterations, both constructors/backends, three alternating before/after process rounds on CPU 2. Every count matched. At 40,000, medians fell from about 2.59 s to 4.64–5.25 ms (whole-process wall time including startup/parsing). The complete VM suite also passed: 25 workloads, four widths, nine retained samples per engine/cell. [Report](../../docs/vm-execution-benchmarks.md#inline-segment-input-lifetime-review-follow-up) and [raw samples, scripts, hashes](../../bench/vm-execution/results/2026-10-05-owned-segment/).
- Tradeoff/limits: arithmetic remains near or faster than 1.x, but some cheap operations incur overhead. Get(Int) is now 2.05× 1.x at width 256; 19/25 cases meet ≤2×, compared with 20/25 previously. Frequency was not locked. This does not fix the pre-existing generic passthrough-retention case noted by the reviewer. The wider scheduler/HTTP benchmarks were not repeated; the overview labels its older snapshot explicitly.
