## 2026-10-07-6681d08-codex-596e6a#F1
- Disposition: fixed
- Change/reason: the swap no longer cleans up the engine. `Engine::rebase` (`crates/shards-core/src/stackless/engine.rs`) builds frames for the new body and moves every own node's state into them: leaves keep their native state, composites their flags (`Once`), call sites their kept frames (components with `Keep` slots, cached invocations; the body those frames run is recorded so the site selects against the installed revision at its next entry, and the `VmCall` pointer is re-pointed at the new site). Own nodes are found through `CompiledFlow::lowered` (`Lowered::inlined`: the node ranges each inlined call brought); the rest must pair up by shard, control and call target (`pair_units`, `rebasable`), which `validate_reload` checks, rejecting with `reload-incompatible` otherwise. Only the nodes of inlined calls start fresh, and a stateless call site may stand where the other body inlines the call. Fresh nodes are instantiated before anything moves; a failure leaves the old body in place. Since no state is lost, no reset is reported or needs admission.
- Evidence: `a_swap_keeps_once_flags_and_component_state_under_reject` (the review's repro under the default Reject policy, plus a `Once`: `once`, `1`, `2`, `3`, report `swapped == [main]`, `reset` empty); `a_swap_waits_for_the_iteration_in_flight_and_keeps_component_state` (stateful counter across a deferred swap); `a_callee_that_stops_being_inlined_keeps_the_callers_state` (an edit past `INLINE_BUDGET` turns the inlined call into a call site mid-run: `101`, `202`, `203`). The two Codex tests were replaced by these exact-outcome versions: theirs accepted either a rejection or a reported reset.

## 2026-10-07-99873f5-claude-0f3dda#F2

## 2026-10-07-6681d08-codex-596e6a#F2
- Disposition: fixed, wider than reported
- Change/reason: (1) an inlined call ends with `Op::Clear` on each slot it used that may hold a heap value (`Type::is_scalar` slots need nothing, so numeric helpers get no extra instruction; a slot the callee's own inlined call already cleared last is skipped); `Clear` moves a value the accumulator still reads instead of copying it. (2) Found while testing the VM path: a flattened `When`/`While`/`If`/`Repeat` kept its saved input in its hidden slot after exit, the same symptom from `aae5c01`; it is cleared at exit the same way (`Flat::release`). (3) A flow that ends other than at its end (failure, `Stop`, `Return`, `Restart`) clears all such slots (`Lowered::released_slots`, in the step's completion and in the leaf child flow path of `dispatch`). (4) Kept call frames, framed and `VmCall`, unset their non-persistent locals at exit (`release_locals`, `vm_call_op`) instead of at the next entry, on success and failure; `CompiledFunction::scratch_slots` is gone.
- Evidence: `a_completed_inlined_call_releases_its_input`, `a_completed_framed_call_releases_its_input` (the review's two cases), `a_completed_vm_call_releases_its_input` (a body past the budget, the second call through the VM; the loop's input is None so the saved-input fix does not mask it), `a_failed_inlined_call_releases_its_input` (failure under `Maybe`), `a_flattened_composite_releases_its_saved_input` (`when`, `if`, `repeat`), and the unit test `inline::tests::clear_unsets_a_slot_and_moves_a_value_the_accumulator_reads` (also under Miri).
