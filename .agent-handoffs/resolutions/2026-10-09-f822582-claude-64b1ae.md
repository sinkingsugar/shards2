## 2026-10-09-a6cb2a5-claude-83b027#F1

It does not: `examples/esp32/build.rs` generates the `values` suite but `src/main.rs` never includes or runs it (since c1ad136, M6), so no `tests/values.rs` test runs on the device. Not changed here; raised with the author.
