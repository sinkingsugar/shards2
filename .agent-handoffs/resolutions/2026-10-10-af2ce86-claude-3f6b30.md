One mechanism addresses all three findings. `Files::resolve` (`crates/shards-lang/src/files.rs`) pushes every name it looks at, found or not, and `Files::contents` reads a file back by that name through the same reader (`FsFiles`: the filesystem; `MemoryFiles`: its map). The session's recording reader (`crates/shards-lang/src/session.rs`, `Recording`) records each name looked at with a fingerprint of what the load found there: length and hash of the exact bytes, or `None` when it could not be read. The watcher's observation (`crates/shards-lang/src/watch.rs`, `Observation`) is the script's text plus those names as they are now (`Session::observe_dependencies`), and after every reload attempt, accepted or rejected, the watcher adopts what the load consumed (`Session::dependencies`) as both its previous and its attempted sample. The `Files` trait changed for hosts that implement it (`read` is now a provided method over `resolve`); `docs/embedding.md` says so.
