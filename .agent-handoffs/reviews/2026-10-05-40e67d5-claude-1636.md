## F2 — P3 The nesting test does not assert that it sits at the limit
- Location: `crates/shards-lang/tests/lang.rs:573` (`nesting_up_to_the_limit_runs_through_every_control_shard`)
- Evidence: the test computes `n = (MAX_FLOW_DEPTH - 2) / levels` from hand-counted levels per wrapper and only asserts that the chain checks and runs. Today each `n` is exactly the maximum (checked with `shards2 check`: `n` ok, `n+1` fails for all six wrappers). If compose's per-wrapper level accounting later drops (for example a shard composing its body without an extra `compose_flow` level, or a change in how `Do` counts), the test keeps passing at a shallower depth than the compose limit allows.
- Impact: the release CI step added in bc25aeb ("Release coroutine stacks (128 KB, as 1.x) must hold flows nested to the compose limit") would stop testing what its comment claims, silently.
- Suggested direction: also assert that `wrapped_chain(n + 1, wrapper)` fails `check` with `too-deep`, so a drift in level counting fails the test.
