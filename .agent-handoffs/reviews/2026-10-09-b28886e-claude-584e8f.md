## F1 — P3 The device budget's heap claim does not match the reported classic ESP32 low-water
- Location: `crates/shards-core/src/compose_time.rs` `EvalLimits::default` (ESP-IDF branch: "well below the classic ESP32's free heap while the frontend composes"); docs/metaprogramming.md §2.5 ("an evaluation allocates at most about 20 KB of the device's heap").
- Evidence: the new record reports a heap low-water of 11,852 B on the classic ESP32 after the suites (14,944 B on `main`), below the ~20 KB peak the ESP-IDF fuel of 20,000 allows by design. The worst case of an evaluation was not exercised on that chip, and which test set the low-water was not isolated, so this is plausible rather than confirmed. The traversal work lists in `Meter::nest` and `text_size` are also uncharged transient allocations on top of the fuel-bounded ones.
- Impact: an evaluation using its whole budget at the frontend's peak could fail allocation (abort) on the classic ESP32 instead of reporting `expansion-budget`.
- Suggested direction: size the classic chip's default fuel from the measured free heap at compose (or per chip), and add a device test that runs an evaluation to its allocation budget.
